The Lulinha
case

Lula's eldest son has appeared for years in accusations, rumors and investigations. He has never been convicted. One investigation was closed, one rumor is false, and three inquiries are open now. This page separates one thing from another.

Case by case

2004–2022Closed

Gamecorp and Oi's R$132 million

The telecom company Oi paid about R$132 million, between 2004 and 2016, to a group of companies linked to Lulinha. In December 2019, the 69th phase of Car Wash investigated whether the payments were in return for acts by Lula and whether part of the money bought the Atibaia farm.

On January 17, 2022, the Federal Court in São Paulo closed the case. The evidence came from cases annulled after the Supreme Court declared Moro biased, and federal prosecutors themselves asked for it to be closed. The merits were not judged.

The Federal Revenue's claim

The Federal Revenue is claiming R$10 million from Lulinha for alleged tax evasion linked to the Oi contracts. It says there is no proof the services were provided. The claim is disputed.

"Lulinha owns Friboi"

He does not. JBS denied it publicly, and press fact-checks found no partnership between him and the group. Lulinha sued a mayor who called him the company's owner.

INSS inquiry committee lifts his bank secrecy

The suspicion, based on messages obtained by the Federal Police, was that he was a hidden partner of the lobbyist known as Careca do INSS. The joint congressional inquiry (CPMI) later rejected the report that involved Lulinha and former ministers of the Lula and Bolsonaro governments.

The three inquiries reported at the Supreme Court

Justice Mendonça authorized two: one on influence peddling linked to Careca do INSS and medical cannabis at the Health Ministry, another on contracts with Dataprev. Justice Flávio Dino authorized a third, on the use of public bodies to favor companies linked to him. None concerns Banco Master.

What they sayThe defense received the inquiries calmly. Lula said that if his son is guilty, he will have to pay.

Careca's transfers to Lulinha's friend

The Federal Police is investigating five transfers of R$300,000 from a consultancy of Careca do INSS to the company of Roberta Luchsinger, a friend of Lulinha. Her defense says she never had anything to do with the INSS deductions. The Federal Police says it cannot prove she knew where the money came from.

Federal Police points to conclusions about Lulinha "without basis"

A Federal Police report, cited by Justice Moraes, says documents prepared for Justice Mendonça gathered references to Lulinha without proof, and that there is no sign of his direct participation in the INSS frauds.

Where the proof ends

What is proven

  • The sources consulted show no conviction of Lulinha.
  • He does not own Friboi.
  • The Gamecorp investigation was closed, without a ruling on the merits.
  • We found no public investigation against him in the Master case in the sources consulted.

What is still open

  • Whether he engaged in influence peddling in his father's government. That is the subject of the three inquiries.
  • Whether he had any connection to the Careca do INSS money that reached his friend's company.
  • The Federal Revenue's R$10 million claim.
