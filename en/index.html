The triplex, from the inside

This is the case that put Lula in prison. Understanding what he was accused of is what separates the arguments that hold up from those that do not.

The accusation

R$3.7 million in benefits from OAS

Federal prosecutors said Lula and Marisa Letícia were the real owners of a triplex apartment in Guarujá. The purchase and the renovation, including a private elevator, would have been bribes from the construction company OAS for Petrobras contracts.

The evidence cited

A 2010 report and the owner of OAS

Moro relied on a 2010 news report that already treated the property as the couple's, and on the testimony of Léo Pinheiro, the owner of OAS. The defense said Pinheiro was talking to get a plea deal.

The defense

The apartment was never his

Lula would only have been a potential customer, to whom the unit was offered in 2014 without him buying it. For the defense, the owner of the property was OAS.

What about money in his account?

The accusation was never about money deposited in Lula's account. It was the apartment and the renovation. When convicting him, Moro ordered about R$16 million in Lula's assets frozen, including R$606,700 in bank accounts, to compensate Petrobras. The point the defense always attacked was a different one: proof that the property was his.

Why the convictions fell

Vaza Jato · Jun 2019

The Intercept publishes conversations between Moro and prosecutor Deltan Dallagnol. They suggest the judge was guiding the prosecution and that Dallagnol had doubts about the evidence hours before the triplex charges. Moro said there was nothing irregular and criticized the hacking that obtained the messages.

InterceptMigalhas · Moro's response
Lack of jurisdiction · Mar 2021

Justice Fachin rules that the Curitiba court had no jurisdiction to try Lula and annuls the four cases there. The full Supreme Court later confirms it. The cases would have to start over in Brasília.

Poder360
Bias · Mar and Jun 2021

The Supreme Court's 2nd Panel rules, 3 to 2, that Moro was biased. In June, the full court upholds the ruling 7 to 4. The ruling covers decisions in the affected cases; the validity of each piece of evidence depends on the scope of the rulings. It does not extend to any evidence from any body.

Migalhas
Statute of limitations · 2021–2022

When restarted in Brasília, the Atibaia farm charges are rejected and the triplex case is closed because the statute of limitations ran out. Neither case was retried on the merits.

CNN Brasil
UN · Apr 2022

The UN Human Rights Committee concludes that Moro and the prosecutors violated Lula's right to an impartial judge, to the presumption of innocence and to privacy, and that barring his candidacy in 2018 was arbitrary. The committee did not rule on whether he committed the crimes.

Poder360
Odebrecht evidence · Sep 2023

Justice Toffoli declares the evidence from Odebrecht's leniency agreement unusable, as it was contaminated, and calls Lula's imprisonment one of the country's greatest judicial errors. The table below separates what this changes for the company and what it changes for Lula.

The company

What Odebrecht confessed

In December 2016, in an agreement with Brazil, the US and Switzerland, it admitted:

  • more than ten years of bribery and bid rigging, since 2001;
  • about US$788 million paid to officials and parties in several countries;
  • fines of at least US$3.5 billion, together with Braskem.

That confession still stands. Toffoli's ruling did not erase it.

Lula

What Lula was accused of using Odebrecht material

  • Donations to the Lula Institute as bribes: suspended in 2021.
  • Land for the Lula Institute: suspended in 2021.
  • BNDES credit line for works in Angola: case dismissed.
  • Renovations at the Atibaia farm, together with OAS: conviction annulled and charges rejected.

None of these accusations ended in a valid conviction. The company's confession does not, by itself, say that Lula received bribes.

Both sides

For the conclusion to be worth anything, it has to survive the best arguments against Lula. These are facts, not opinions.

Three levels of court

Before the annulment, the triplex conviction was upheld by the TRF-4 and, unanimously, by the STJ's 5th Panel, which only reduced the sentence. The annulment came on procedural grounds, not from a new assessment of the evidence.

Agência Brasil
Merits

Only 3 of the 11 cases ended in acquittal on the merits. Jurists disagree on whether annulment and the statute of limitations are equivalent to acquittal. In technical language, they are not.

Petrobras

The corruption at the state oil company existed. In 2015, Petrobras recognized R$6.2 billion in losses from embezzlement in its 2014 accounts. Odebrecht confessed to paying bribes, as the table above shows.

Where the proof ends

What is proven

  • The Curitiba convictions were annulled. In the September 2022 snapshot, there was no valid conviction in the 11 cases presented; this is not an update of every case in 2026.
  • The Supreme Court declared Moro biased and the Curitiba court without jurisdiction to try him.
  • Lula spent 580 days in prison for a conviction that was later annulled.
  • In three cases, Lula was acquitted on the merits.
  • The corruption at Petrobras existed, and PT leaders were convicted in the mensalão.

What no ruling has stated

  • That Lula proved he was innocent on the merits of the triplex and the farm. The cases were annulled and ran out the statute of limitations.
  • That the evidence was forged. The rulings speak of bias, lack of jurisdiction and contaminated evidence.
  • That Lula received bribes. The conviction that said so no longer exists.
